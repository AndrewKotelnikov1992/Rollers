# Концепты корпуса Rollers

Созданы встроенным image_gen по README.md и изображениям SolidWorks. Это визуальные концепты, не проверенная CAD-конструкция.

- champagne-concept.png — мягко скруглённый сатиновый корпус цвета шампань, вид на ноге и открытая компоновка.
- light-gray-concept.png — более строгий светло-серый корпус, нижний вход и платформа в рабочем положении.

Для дальнейшей проработки предлагается сочетать форму champagne с нижним входом light-gray. В первом изображении видимый вырез для петли не задаёт достаточный проход для колёс: нижний торец должен быть открыт по габариту сборки. Размеры, положение ретракторов, крепление тросов, направляющие перехода из горизонтального положения в вертикальное и снятие носка с упора нужно проверить в CAD; изображения не подтверждают работоспособность этих узлов. Чёрные контактные подушки на разрезе показаны условно; носок с ответными магнитами находится под брюками.

## Точный промпт A

Use case: product-mockup. Create a refined industrial design presentation board with photorealistic 3D renders of a wearable retractable roller platform housing. Input images are engineering references: bare assembly stored vertically along front/outer shin and deployed horizontally under shoe. Preserve the essential topology: ONE narrow platform with TWO inline wheels, one each end, telescopic middle rails. Housing stays vertically on shin OUTSIDE tailored trousers, held by two hidden rubber coated magnets to an elastic sleeve UNDER trousers; no visible external straps. Two spring cable retractors inside upper housing pull platform back through bottom entry. Not a backpack, not a shoe with built in wheels. Design concept A: tall slim rounded rectangular champagne satin shell, subtly convex face, generous radiused edges, dark warm gray soft back pad, fine perimeter seam, no logos, no LEDs, no ornament. Realistic volume to fully enclose both wheels and platform; overall shell approximately lower shin length with enough wheel depth, not impossibly paper thin. Small flat textile pull loop emerges from lower opening reachable by opposite shoe. Housing bottom stops above ankle to leave shoe movement. Board with 3 large clean views on warm off-white: left large hero of single housing worn on side-front of lower leg, charcoal tailored trousers and black leather business shoe, closed/stowed, wheels invisible; center isolated three-quarter shell showing tasteful champagne material and bottom entry with soft dark guide lips; right technical product cutaway of same design showing vertically nested two wheel platform, upper pair of retractor reels, two fine cables, rails, and hidden attachment pads. Clear considered proportions matching references, restrained premium office accessory, subtle grounded shadows, soft studio lighting. No words or labels. Do not add extra wheels, knee brace, big straps, boot, motors. Concept render, not an engineering blueprint.

## Точный промпт B

Use case: product-mockup. Generate a second industrial design concept for the wearable retractable skate in the conversation engineering reference images. A minimal LIGHT GRAY satin polymer housing with subtly chamfered long edges, straight precise sides, rounded corners, discreet graphite rear cushioning, removable rear service panel, no branding. Same functional concept as champagne render but improve BOTTOM ENTRY: entire shell cross section open underneath wide and deep enough for complete wheel and platform to pass; show this explicitly in an isolated underside 3/4 view. Shell is shin-length and encloses the full two wheel telescoping assembly in stowed position, mounted OUTSIDE business trousers by two hidden magnetic pads on sleeve beneath cloth. Two spring cable reels at top, platform drawn down with black textile loop by opposite foot; two inline wheels only, one front and one rear. Create a coherent 3-panel photoreal design board on clean pale gray studio background: left large stowed shell worn vertically on lower outer/front shin over dark navy tailored trousers with black leather derby shoe; middle same isolated shell tilted to reveal genuinely full-width bottom mouth, dark replaceable guide inserts and loop; right deployed state same leg and empty shell still on shin while black business shoe stands on the extracted horizontal slim two-wheel platform underneath, low rounded toe stop cup overlapping tip of shoe to retain toe without straps; two fine retracting cables visibly connect lower shell opening to platform. Shell must not extend down across ankle; no enormous boot, no extra wheels, no powered parts, no decorative LEDs. Reference engineering geometry is two wheels at opposite ends of adjustable long platform. Side profile/right three-quarter view deployed so both wheels and housing clearly readable. Keep mechanism plausible as design exploration. Strong clean silhouettes, premium understated office accessory, high-end studio product rendering. No text.
